The Founder

Meet Jithu Bist
A career built around
hair, fashion and people.

Salon Squeeze was started by Jithu Bist to provide hair services clients love and return for. His journey reflects dedication to craft, continuous learning, and a genuine care for every client who sits in the chair.

Jithu began his hairstyling career in 2000 in Sri Lanka with Ramani Fernando, later trained with TONI&GUY in the UK, and spent over 12 years working on fashion events with fashion guru Prasad Bidappa. His professional work has included styling associated with personalities such as Deepika Padukone, Anushka Sharma, Anil Kumble, Jacqueline Fernandez, Chiyaan Vikram and Rahul Dravid, among others.
